04 / CREATIVE PROCESS

The work behind the sound.

Musical ideas get stronger through listening, rewriting, rehearsal-minded decisions, and carefully balancing the ensemble.

01 / COMPOSITION

Musical ideas

Develop themes, harmonic direction, contrasts, and pacing that make sense over a full show.

02 / ORCHESTRATION

Color and balance

Think about the roles of woodwinds, brass, battery, and front ensemble rather than treating each section identically.

03 / REVISION

Refine through listening

Revisit exposed entrances, transitions, percussion balance, and material that repeats too often.

04 / PERFORMANCE

Keep it playable

Consider clarity, endurance, rehearsal needs, and how complex writing translates into actual ensemble performance.
