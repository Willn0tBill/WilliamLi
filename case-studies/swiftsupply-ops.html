01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What is it?

SwiftSupply Ops is an internal management platform connecting vending, power-washing services, and personal selling. It organizes operational records that would otherwise be spread across separate trackers and workflows.

The public screenshot shows the sign-in interface only. Business data and staff-only screens are intentionally not shown on this portfolio.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

The problem to solve

The underlying business lines have different requirements: vending needs stock and service logs; service work needs scheduling and job status; selling needs fast inventory and transaction records.

A single application must keep these concerns distinct enough to stay usable while allowing authorized staff to view the information relevant to their role.

03 / APPROACH

Designing the solution

The repository describes a mobile-first dashboard and role-based access for Owner, Manager, Employee, and Viewer. Workflows cover appointments, stock, service visits, payments, and ledger records.

It also includes data-sync infrastructure and a Google Sheets bridge with queued updates, rather than assuming all external connections complete immediately.

05 / IMPLEMENTATION

Behind the build

The README documents Supabase authorization rules and an inactive-by-default staff approval flow, as well as a responsive mobile layout and installable PWA.

A Google Sheets synchronization bridge and related queues are documented. The public case study intentionally excludes internal account details, customer records, and operational metrics.
