01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

What is it?

SwiftSupply is a public-facing site for snacks, drinks, orders, and related services. It pairs a recognizable brand with practical paths to shop, browse bulk options, and find business information.

Unlike SwiftSupply Ops, which is an internal management tool, this project focuses on the customer’s first impression and navigation.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

The problem to solve

A small business website may need to explain the offering, introduce products, and make the next action straightforward without overwhelming visitors.

When products and services share one brand, the information structure needs to help customers find the right path.

03 / APPROACH

Designing the solution

The homepage uses a concise value proposition and direct calls to action, while the navigation separates Shop, Bulk, Alerts, About, and Power Washing.

Product photography anchors the front page, with a clear difference between shopping and service information.

04 / KEY CAPABILITIES

What the project includes

01

Clear customer paths

Prominent links to shop, learn about products, and explore additional services.

02

Category-aware navigation

Distinct routes for bulk orders, stock alerts, and business information.

03

Visual brand

A recognizable dark storefront identity with product-focused imagery.

04

Companion operations tool

The portfolio distinguishes this public website from the staff-only SwiftSupply Ops platform.

05 / IMPLEMENTATION

Behind the build

The repository exposes separate pages and stylesheet layers for storefront features and power-washing services. The homepage screenshot confirms the public branding and main navigation.

No claims about transaction volume, fulfillment automation, or payment processing are made here because those would require additional evidence.

06 / CURRENT STATE

What’s available today

A publicly accessible storefront website. Functionality behind specific ordering and payment flows was not independently validated during the portfolio update.

A verified, step-by-step customer journey—from product selection through the actual order handoff—would be a useful next addition to this case study.
